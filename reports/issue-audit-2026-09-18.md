# HyperGAN complete issue audit

Audit date: 2026-09-18, America/Denver. Scope: **all 112 GitHub issues, including 17 open and 95 closed, and all 299 discussion comments**. Pull requests were excluded; their preservation/disposition is covered by the [preservation report](resurrection-preservation-2026-09-18.md). Three subagents independently reviewed disjoint batches of 38/37/37 issues, then the coordinator reconciled recommendations against the merged CPU foundation and release plan.

The goal is a usable next release built on mature GAN research. Historical questions remain searchable, but obsolete APIs and unqualified research integrations do not remain active commitments. A closed old issue does not establish that the new runtime implements its use case.

## Disposition policy

- **Keep open:** a concrete unmet requirement with a place in the release workflow. Add a scoped comment and acceptance checklist; preserve the original report.
- **Close completed:** the reported capability has evidence on unreleased `develop`. State that this does not repair historical PyPI wheels or establish broader platform/GPU support.
- **Close superseded:** the affected implementation is retired or its request is replaced by a named contract. Preserve useful regression requirements and link the migration path. Use GitHub's `not_planned` reason rather than claiming the old code was fixed.
- **Close deferred:** the request is outside the committed release slice. Retain its product hypothesis or admission criterion without promising an integration. Use `not_planned`.
- **Leave closed:** assess the full discussion for missed requirements, record the result below, and avoid reopening or posting noise to historical conversations.
